[b]Splits RimWorld's broadest work types into focused jobs, so you can assign specialists instead of generalists.[/b]

[h2]New work types[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Nurse[/b] covers rescuing, taking patients to bed for operations, feeding colonists, prisoners and animals, hemogen, and visiting the sick, while Doctors keep tending and surgery.
[*][b]Butcher[/b] covers butcher table bills, for meat and kibble.
[*][b]Deconstruct[/b] covers deconstructing, uninstalling, clearing space for blueprints, filling in pits, and removing floors, roofs and foundations.
[*][b]Repair[/b] covers repairing and fixing broken-down buildings, while Construction keeps building.
[/list]

[h2]Tidier Work tab[/h2]
[list]
[*]Fishing sits next to Hunting, and plant cutting next to Growing.
[*]Dark study joins Research.
[*]Smoothing is Construction's lowest job, walls before floors.
[*]New colonies start with manual priorities turned on.
[/list]

[h2]Compatibility[/h2]
These mods' jobs are folded in rather than left as separate columns:
[list]
[*][b]Hospitality[/b]: Recruiter and Diplomat join Warden.
[*][b]Therapy[/b]: therapy joins Warden.
[*][b]Simple Improve[/b]: improving joins Construction as its lowest job.
[*][b]Recycle This[/b]: recycling and destroying join Crafting.
[*][b]Vanilla Books Expanded[/b]: writing moves next to Art.
[*][b]Dubs Bad Hygiene[/b]: patient care joins Nurse.
[*][b]Vehicle Framework[/b]: vehicle repair and disassembly join Repair and Deconstruct.
[*][b]VE Gravship[/b]: gravship maintenance joins Repair.
[*][b]Guarding Pawns + Keyz Allow Utilities[/b]: finishing off joins guard duty.
[/list]
Not compatible with Complex Jobs, Nurse Job, or Smooth Walls then Floor, because they change the same work types.
Safe to add to an existing save.
