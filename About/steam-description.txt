Splits RimWorld's broadest work types into smaller ones and reorders the Work tab. No new mechanics, no settings menu.

[h1]New work types[/h1]
[list]
[*][b]Nurse[/b] — rescuing, feeding colonists, prisoners and animals, hemogen, visiting the sick. Out of Doctor and Warden.
[*][b]Butcher[/b] — butcher table bills. Out of Cooking.
[*][b]Deconstruct[/b] — deconstruct, uninstall, remove floors, roofs and foundations. Out of Construction.
[*][b]Repair[/b] — repair, fix broken-down buildings. Out of Construction.
[*]Doctor keeps all tending and surgery. Construction keeps building.
[*]Both Doctor and Nurse can carry a patient to bed for an operation.
[/list]

[h1]Work tab[/h1]
[list]
[*]Fishing moved next to Hunting.
[*]Plant cutting moved next to Growing.
[*]Dark study folded into Research as its first job.
[*]Smoothing demoted within Construction; walls before floors.
[*]New colonies default to manual priorities. Existing saves keep your setting.
[/list]

[h1]Mechanoids and children[/h1]
[list]
[*]Constructoid gains Deconstruct and Repair, Fabricor gains Butcher, Paramedic gains Nurse.
[*]Nurse from age 10, Butcher from 7, Repair from 10. Deconstruct has no age limit.
[/list]

[h1]Mod support[/h1]
Folded into existing columns, only when the mod is present. Detected by job, not by mod name, so forks and continued versions work.
[list]
[*][b]Hospitality[/b] — recruiting and entertaining guests join Warden.
[*][b]Therapy[/b] — joins Warden.
[*][b]Simple Improve[/b] — improving joins Construction as its lowest job.
[*][b]Recycle This[/b] — recycle and destroy join Crafting.
[*][b]Dubs Bad Hygiene[/b] — washing, bedpans and fluids join Nurse.
[*][b]Vanilla Books Expanded[/b] — writing moved next to Art.
[*][b]Vehicle Framework[/b] — vehicle repair and disassembly join Repair and Deconstruct.
[*][b]Vanilla Gravship Expanded[/b] — gravship maintenance joins Repair.
[*][b]Guarding Pawns[/b] — Keyz Allow Utilities' finish off joins guard duty.
[/list]

[h1]Compatibility[/h1]
[list]
[*]Safe to add mid-save. Removing it resets the affected work priorities.
[*]Supports all DLC, requires none.
[*]Incompatible with Complex Jobs, Nurse Job, and Smooth Walls First.
[/list]
