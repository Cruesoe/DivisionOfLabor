Splits RimWorld's broadest work types into focused jobs, so you can assign specialists instead of generalists.

[h1]New work types[/h1]
[list]
[*][b]Nurse[/b] — rescuing, feeding colonists, prisoners and animals, hemogen, and visiting the sick. Doctors keep tending and surgery.
[*][b]Butcher[/b] — butcher table bills, for meat and kibble.
[*][b]Deconstruct[/b] — deconstructing, uninstalling, and removing floors, roofs and foundations.
[*][b]Repair[/b] — repairing and fixing broken-down buildings. Construction keeps building.
[/list]

[h1]Tidier Work tab[/h1]
[list]
[*]Fishing sits next to Hunting, and plant cutting next to Growing.
[*]Dark study joins Research.
[*]Smoothing is Construction's lowest job, walls before floors.
[*]New colonies start with manual priorities turned on.
[/list]

[h1]Supported mods[/h1]
Folded in rather than left as separate columns:
[list]
[*][b]Hospitality[/b] — Recruiter and Diplomat join Warden.
[*][b]Therapy[/b] — therapy joins Warden.
[*][b]Simple Improve[/b] — improving joins Construction as its lowest job.
[*][b]Recycle This[/b] — recycling and destroying join Crafting.
[*][b]Vanilla Books Expanded[/b] — writing moves next to Art.
[*][b]Dubs Bad Hygiene[/b] — patient care joins Nurse.
[*][b]Vehicle Framework[/b] — vehicle repair and disassembly join Repair and Deconstruct.
[*][b]VE Gravship[/b] — gravship maintenance joins Repair.
[*][b]Guarding Pawns + Keyz Allow Utilities[/b] — finishing off joins guard duty.
[/list]

[h1]Compatibility[/h1]
Safe to add to an existing save. Incompatible with Complex Jobs, Nurse Job, and Smooth Walls then Floor.
